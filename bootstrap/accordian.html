A story of FISH"S LIFE.

A "fish's life story" typically follows a remarkable biological journey through six main developmental stages from an egg to an adult.
Key Life Stages
Egg: A female fish lays tiny eggs in plants or rocks, often protected by a jelly-like coating while an embryo develops inside.
Larvae: The baby fish hatches looking very tiny and weak, surviving off an attached yolk sac for food.
Fry: The young fish absorbs its yolk sac, begins swimming actively, and starts hunting for tiny plankton on its own.
Juvenile: Scales and stronger fins form, allowing the fish to look and swim like a miniature adult while dodging predators.
Adult: The fully grown fish reaches maturity, joins schools, and prepares to migrate or spawn.
Spawning: The adult lays or fertilizes new eggs to restart the natural cycle of life.

The new journey.

A New Journey by Samantha Kannan is a memoir that follows a young American graduate as she moves to a village in southern India to teach and transform her life.
Key Plot PointsThe Struggle:
Jurnee is a recent college graduate who cannot find a teaching job in the United States and feels discouraged.The Opportunity: She accepts an unexpected chance to teach at a school in a village in Kerala, India.The Culture Shock: She overcomes personal anxiety, self-doubt, and major cultural differences while adjusting to her new home.The Adventures: She narrowly escapes an arranged marriage, learns how cashews grow, encounters a king cobra, and finds unexpected love.

Pippa's Night parade.

Pippa's Night Parade is a 2019 children's picture book written by Lisa Robinson and illustrated by Lucy Fleming about a imaginative young girl who overcomes her bedtime fears using creativity and fashion.
The Plot and Conflict:
A Big Imagination: Pippa has a very active imagination. During the day, she wears creative play-armor like masks and scarves.
Nighttime Monsters: When night falls, villains, dragons, bears, and pirates crawl out of her storybooks.
Failed Fixes: Pippa tries night-lights, triple knots on her blankets, disguises, and running to her parents' room, but the monsters keep coming.
